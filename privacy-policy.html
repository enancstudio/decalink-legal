Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the current release of Decalink handles information when you play the game on mobile or web.

Last updated: July 3, 2026 Language: English Scope: current live release
Terms of Use Support

1. Who operates Decalink

Decalink is published under the Decalink brand. In this Privacy Policy, “we”, “us”, and “our” refer to the publisher of Decalink.

2. Information handled by the current release

The current live release stores gameplay progress, settings, tutorial completion, and daily challenge state locally on your device so the game can remember your progress.

The current live release does not require account registration. Core gameplay data is stored locally. Optional purchase flows may involve platform account and transaction data handled by your app store provider and our purchase infrastructure. The mobile release may also use analytics and rewarded advertising services described below.

3. How local data is used

  • To save your settings, such as sound and accessibility preferences.
  • To remember completed levels and other local gameplay progress.
  • To preserve daily challenge completion and similar gameplay state.
  • To keep the app functional between sessions on the same device.

4. Third-party services

The current live release uses a purchase infrastructure for optional in-app purchases (for example, Supporter Pack purchase and restore) through platform billing channels. This may involve store account identifiers, transaction metadata, and entitlement status required to validate or restore purchases.

The current live release may use Firebase Analytics to understand gameplay and monetization events, such as level completion, Daily Challenge starts, rewarded ad funnel events, and Supporter Pack purchase or restore events.

The current live release may use Google AdMob to show rewarded advertising when a player chooses to watch an ad for an extra hint. Ad requests may involve device, advertising, diagnostic, and approximate location signals handled by Google according to its own policies and the choices available on your device or in the app store region.

The current live release does not include a dedicated crash reporting SDK. If a future release adds Crashlytics or another crash reporting service, this Privacy Policy and store privacy disclosures will be updated.

5. Sharing and selling of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data with analytics, advertising, purchase service providers, and platform operators only as needed to operate the app, measure app performance, show rewarded ads, process or restore purchases, or where disclosure is required by applicable law.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because the current live release stores gameplay-related data locally on your device, that data remains on your device until you remove the app, clear app data, or otherwise delete the relevant local storage.

Purchase records and related billing data are retained by the relevant platform and purchase infrastructure providers under their own policies.

Analytics, advertising, and purchase service providers may retain service data under their own retention policies.

7. International users and legal rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable privacy laws, including rights to access, delete, or object to certain processing of personal information. The current live release is designed to minimize account-level data by keeping core gameplay state on your device and avoiding account registration.

8. Children

Decalink is not directed to children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
