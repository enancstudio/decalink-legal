Support

Use this page for gameplay help, purchase restore guidance, and privacy-related support directions for Decalink.

Last updated: May 19, 2026 International release support
Privacy Policy Terms of Use

1. General help

If you need help with gameplay, progression, settings, or a technical issue, please use the support contact channel listed on the official Decalink app store listing for your platform.

2. Purchases and restore

  • Use the in-app “Restore Purchase” action if it is available for your build.
  • Make sure you are signed in with the same platform account used for the purchase.
  • For billing problems or refunds, contact Apple App Store Support or Google Play Support, because payment processing is generally handled by the platform.

3. Privacy and local data

The current live release stores gameplay-related data locally on your device. If you want to remove that data, delete the app or clear the app data from your device settings, where supported.

For privacy details, please read the Privacy Policy.

4. What to include in a support request

  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • The app version shown in the store or build you installed.
  • A short description of the issue and how to reproduce it.
  • Screenshots or screen recordings if they help explain the problem.

5. Response route

Support requests should be sent through the publisher contact method shown on the official store listing for Decalink. That route should be treated as the primary support contact for the released app.

This support page is intended to be bundled with the app website and linked from the in-app settings screen, App Store Connect, and Google Play listing materials.
